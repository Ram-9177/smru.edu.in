# International citations — SMRU (for the marketing team)

Get SMRU listed on the international education directories and portals below, each carrying the
**standard name** and the **bridge sentence**. These are the off-site twins of the domestic
aggregator work (Phase 7); together they build the international-entity signal.

## Standard copy to paste (do not alter)

- **Name:** St. Mary's University (SMRU), Hyderabad
- **Legal name:** St. Mary's Rehabilitation University
- **Bridge sentence:** *St. Mary's University (SMRU) is the public name of St. Mary's Rehabilitation
  University, a UGC-recognised private university in Hyderabad, Telangana, established under
  Telangana Ordinance No. 2 of 2025 and Telangana Act No. 10 of 2026.*
- **Website:** https://smru.edu.in · **International admissions:** https://smru.edu.in/international/
- **Apply:** https://apply.smru.edu.in

## Directories and portals to claim / list on

| Target | Why it matters | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **StudyInIndia** (Govt. of India portal, studyinindia.gov.in) | Genuine authority link; the official inbound-student gateway | Register the institution; list programmes and the international page |
| **Bachelorsportal / Mastersportal (StudyPortals)** | High-traffic international course search | Claim the profile; list programmes with the international page as the apply route |
| **AIU membership listing** (aiu.ac.in) | Equivalence authority international students check | Confirm the listing shows the legal name + website |
| **studyabroad.shiksha** | Indian-study portal read by international applicants | Claim/align the listing to the standard name |
| **Yocket** | Large international-applicant community | Create/claim the profile |
| **Leverage Edu** | Study-abroad counselling audience | Request a listing/profile |
| **EDUopinions** | International student reviews | Create the profile; invite genuine reviews |
| **ICCR** (where applicable, iccr.gov.in) | Scholarship/exchange pathway for some countries | Confirm eligibility and listing |
| **Country-specific agent directories** (Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nigeria, Kenya, UAE, Oman) | Where in-country applicants search | Provide the standard copy to authorised agents |

## Rules

- Every listing links to `https://smru.edu.in/` and, for applicants, `https://smru.edu.in/international/`.
- Never claim a foreign licence outcome — link the relevant `/carebridge/{destination}/` page, which
  carries the "registration is granted by the foreign regulator, not SMRU" disclaimer.
- Never publish a fee figure that is not on the official site.
- Keep the name exactly as above so engines reconcile every citation to one entity.
